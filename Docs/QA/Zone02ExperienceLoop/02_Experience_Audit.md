## Emotional pacing hypothesis

Ratings represent a designer's intended/likely tension contribution, not measured emotion. Normality1 → Radar2 → knocks3 → scrape4 → fieldwork/installation2 → reflection3 → long recovery1–2. The strongest visible escalation occurs during practical computer use; its aftermath returns to the previous loop. Earlier events mostly do not change the interpretation of subsequent voluntary stops or observations. This makes the chapter feel like separated demonstrations despite the compatible audio motif.

No replacement creature art or external package is necessary to address orchestration. A bounded painted-root/particle motion layer would be a resource opportunity; it must not deform actual map geometry, contacts, readable controls or archived photographs.

Common scale:1 absent/broken;2 isolated or inconsistent;3 coherent and perceptible with limitations;4 sustained/contextual with tested recovery;5 consistently effective across representative human playtests. A5 cannot be established here. These are design assessments.

## Prioritized problems

1. P0 gameplay blocker: forward corridor auto-returned to Zone01. Confirmed/repaired separately; preserve its failed baseline evidence.
2. P1 presentation durability: `Once` commits at request start; closing audience or pausing before a delayed visual can consume the only opportunity. Static causal evidence is concrete; interrupted-window reproduction belongs in functional validation.
3. P1 coherent aftermath: repeated normal navigation after a dramatic beat gives little reason to reinterpret a voluntary engine stop or later observation as being followed.
4. P1 opportunity chain: Radar→knocks→scrape depends on specific audience and all expire at Hull install. This completed continuation exposed them, but another normal route can skip them. Avoid fabricated certainty about how common that is.
5. P2 anticipation/motion: tiny reflection and static normal imagery offer little visual preparation. Preserve actual data and painted style.
6. P2 quiet semantics: quietTime counts during running drama, while root recurrence is regular. Silence/recovery should be measured from cessation rather than request start.
