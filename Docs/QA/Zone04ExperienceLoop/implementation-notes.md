# Zone04 delivery implementation notes

Source implementation for the single redesign in `03_Horror_Redesign.md`. This note covers only the presentation controller/state and the fragment exposure probe. Native compile, tests, images, listener recordings and the final voyage are owned by the parent validation pass; no Unity operation was performed for this source handoff.

- `ZoneFourPresentationController` replaces launch receipts with one local owned cue and delivery checkpoint. `PendingCue` and `ExperienceStage` are passive read-only diagnostics. Existing `fragment`, `hidden.a`, `hidden.b`, `final.signal` receipts still suppress replay.
- Fragment delivery requires the actual Mission Log card, both exact coordinate strings, completed processing, alpha at least .9 and .25 unscaled seconds across consecutive Canvas render frames. It does not require the 1.48-second recovery voice to overlap the completed card at 2.5 seconds.
- Hidden A/B require the actual selected archived image and its site binding, the Photo Lab surface, category/SFX/output permissions, an enabled listener, and a live unmuted production-clip source. Delivery takes at least .65 seconds of both real voice-clock progress and wall time over consecutive rendered frames. A voice reset/change, a missing frame, or a voice-clock stall longer than .2 seconds restarts observation. These probes establish a bounded presentation opportunity, not human hearing, recognition or a complete nine-second listen.
- Final opportunity is earned from observed position and voyage-distance movement across distinct frames, with speed above .1 on the unobstructed Helm inside the already unlocked 360-unit approach. `ZoneFourPresentationState.FinalSignalOpportunity` is `final.signal.opportunity` in the existing played list. Its receipt survives Continue/Rest and allows stationary retry. Final delivery additionally requires the actual active water tint and observed audio onset after the real 1.2-second offset, followed by .65 seconds of voice/wall exposure. An onset first observed more than .2 seconds into the voice does not certify its arrival. Presentation does not select an ending.
- Audience loss, focus loss, pause, modal/delete confirmation, transition or direct interaction cancels owned tails before delivery. The same Mission Log/photo/earned final opportunity can retry without Scan, shutter, SEND, capture or cost. Launch failure/interruption uses .5-second wall backoff. Failed opportunity/receipt publication retries at one-second wall intervals, independent of fixture `Tick(seconds)`.
- Once delivery was observed, a failed receipt write retains `delivered-awaiting-publication` in the same live controller/timeline, including through pause, focus loss, audience changes and natural tail completion. It retries publication without replay. Actual timeline restoration clears local pending/future effects and uses the restored snapshot's receipts and gameplay opportunity. Rest keeps the live pending state; later Continue uses durable receipts, capture/photo state and the earned final opportunity.
- Storage boundary: if storage continues failing and the controller is destroyed, the process crashes, or Continue reloads before the delivered receipt becomes durable, local delivery cannot be recovered. The saved opportunity may replay. Exactly-once delivery across that failure/reload boundary is not promised. A Journal rollback intentionally removes future delivery/opportunity that is absent from its checkpoint.
- During hidden/final primary, the existing sparse-water environmental target is capped at .12 (the existing .48 multiplier yields a .0576 target gain before mixer settings), with its existing 900–7800 Hz filter interpolation and four-second gain fade. After the owned sequence stops, the cap holds for four real seconds, then the original spatial target/fade resumes. Distant structure is stopped and its eligibility timer held through primary/recovery. Machinery, controls, minigame, warnings and outside effects retain their existing buses/authority.

No EffectDirector/EffectSequence framework, audio asset, package, scene, map/mission asset, photo compositor, gameplay transaction or ending authority changed in this source handoff. Static inspection and `git diff --check` completed; native behavior remains to be validated by the root operator.
