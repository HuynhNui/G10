Creature06 is PHOTO and manual SEND only. Taking a picture does not complete the photo objective or grant capture material. The existing `ZoneThreeMissionTests.ThreeSitesUseManualSendAndBothCapturedMaterialsAreConsumedOnlyByCraft` exercises those distinctions through the actual shutter, Photo Lab SEND and capture flow, although its `Place` helper restores a position and refills resources; it does not establish terrain-route usability or whole-voyage pacing.

The first captured material grants the rock-breaker recipe. CRAFT consumes one `Z3_Creature_01` and one `Z3_Creature_03`; INSTALL has no second material cost (mission lines 98–125). `ZoneOneStory.ApplyProgressionAction` publishes the action before notifying presentation or starting the committed rock sequence (lines 215–228). Failed publication is covered by `FailedCraftPublicationRestoresCargoReceiptsAndPresentationNotification`.

The current map keeps entry `(11,97)` at 230 m; L1 `(446.08,214.45714)` at 360 m; L2 `(48,630.56573)` at 470 m; and L3/POI l4 `(1114.08,249.48)` at 640 m. The physical rock is `(1480,150)`, radius 70, at 590 m with the story's ±10 m tolerance. After destruction, the physical exit is `(1750,25)`, radius 75, destination Zone04. The return trigger `(25,25)`, radius 75, retains its intentional entry overlap. Source review does not authorize changing these coordinates, route rules or trigger semantics.

`RockBreakerSequence` remains presentation over an already committed objective/world flag. Its terrain reveal is deferred until 1.5 seconds and the gameplay lock is ten seconds (lines 19–21, 32–43, 66–68). Pause stops sequence presentation; resume starts the authored sequence at elapsed time, so expired detonations are not replayed (lines 46–53). Completion may leave the bounded 13.2-second authored audio sequence tail running after the ten-second lock; explicit stop cancels it (lines 83–91). The exit must still be physically reached.

## Hypotheses to investigate after the voyage

1. **Opportunity and exposure:** L2 radar requires an additional actual charged scan after SEND. If a player proceeds straight to Helm or already knows the route, neither radar receipt nor its downstream passage/reflection becomes eligible. Check the real route/actions before concluding that a missing cue is broken. Cues should earn their exposure through existing task behavior if a redesign is warranted.
2. **Committed versus perceived:** `Once` starts a sequence and publishes its receipt immediately (controller lines 170–176; `ExpeditionLoop.CommitZoneThreeCue` lines 69–78). A later modal, direct interaction, capture or rock event makes `Unobstructed` false and the sequence condition cancels it. Pause/focus suspension also clears owned transients; the durable receipt survives. Consequently a receipt proves an accepted trigger, not that the later peak was displayed/heard in full. Check actual trigger-to-peak exposure and cancellation evidence.
3. **Task attention:** Primary beats share `effects.ActiveSequenceCount` as their busy gate, but local Photo Lab cues and durable assembly callbacks are independent. A rapid panel change, SEND or consecutive craft/install input may reduce exposure or cause overlap/replacement. Confirm from actual frame/audio evidence; do not assume all technically valid effects reached the player.
4. **Whole-route pacing:** L1→L2→L3 has substantial horizontal/depth travel. Historical tests restore positions and/or substitute open water to isolate cues. They verify conditions and authority but cannot settle real terrain navigation, resource budget or the ratio of task friction to horror. Baseline and final must retain physical navigation and distinguish execution delay from designed waiting.
5. **Persistent acoustic change:** After 100-distance passage, the pre-break stone layer remains at a low target rather than returning after the reflection. If most remaining travel occurs in that state, the early mineral identity may be reduced for a long interval. Evaluate whether this supports tension or flattens it using actual listening/exposure, without treating RMS as emotion.
6. **Safety/readability of scoped visuals:** The surface creates one reused graphic/material on demand, uses the authored local composition parent and disables drawing when restored. It does not have its own Update/coroutine or a screen readback. The current pixel/occlusion tests support visibility boundaries and cleanup; they do not establish perceptual quality, active GPU cost or human fear.

## Conditional implementation design: interrupted primary beats

**Conditional only:** use this section if the completed baseline demonstrates a primary cue being accepted but missing its meaningful presentation because of panel/focus/pause interruption. It is not a redesign decision, an implemented fix or a claim that an interruption occurred in recorded gameplay. The bounded candidate below concerns `engine`, `radar` and `reflection`; specimen reactions, assembly and committed demolition retain their distinct authority/lifecycle.

### Exact API constraints and seams

- `EffectDirector.PlaySequence` (lines 99–116) returns an accepted handle. The production Zone03 sequence assets currently have parent target `Global`; child effects use their own Radar/Reflection/PhotoLab targets. A hidden or rejected visual child does not invalidate its Global parent. The controller must gate the *required child audience*, not merely the parent handle.
- `IsActive(handle)` (lines 60–61) reports presence, not why a sequence ended. `TryGetSequenceTime` (62–68) exists only while the sequence remains registered. In `Tick` (246–281), natural duration, unavailable parent, lost audio clock and failed condition all remove the same sequence without an outcome callback. **Do not commit a deferred receipt merely because the handle disappeared.** A hitch can also skip an expired cue in `AdvanceSequence` (124–154).
- Existing APIs are sufficient for a smaller *delivery-checkpoint* policy: `TryGetSequenceTime`, `TryGetAudioTime` (73–79) and the actual required surface's `visual.procedural.ActivePreset`/`Visible`. `IsPlaying` alone only proves a request. `TryGetAudioTime` observes a real pooled voice, but it can report clip length for a voice that just stopped before executor cleanup. Neither API proves human perception, audible device output or a rendered frame. Receipt semantics must be described as a verified presentation checkpoint, with actual final footage/audio verifying the experience.
- `EffectSurface.Available` (19–30) checks active hierarchy and optional CanvasGroup alpha. It does not check app focus, foreground sibling occlusion or `CurrentPanel`. `UIManager.OpenPanel` (79–105) deactivates the previous station; `CabinStationView.Open` (333–344) brakes before changing panels. Today that brake can satisfy the engine stop-edge condition after Helm has already been left. Guard engine eligibility with Helm, rather than letting a station change consume it.
- The current `Unobstructed` condition (controller line 44) is shared by all primary sequences and does not include Helm/Radar for the entire playback. Add a controller-local audience predicate: focused, safe/unobstructed, director unsuppressed, correct `CurrentPanel`, and required real surface available. Engine/reflection require Helm (engine also actual stopped propulsion for its stop-edge meaning); radar requires Radar. Do **not** require `Radar.IsScanning` throughout playback: it is the initial gameplay eligibility, while the secondary response may legitimately outlast the scan. No automatic panel opening, input lock, scan, charge or navigation mutation is needed.

### Smallest bounded lifecycle candidate

Keep one transient primary-cue slot in `ZoneThreePresentationController` (kind, authored sequence, required audience, handle, original observation distance, stage). A single slot plus the existing busy gate prevents per-frame duplicate launches and competing primary retries. Split the current sequence overload of `Once` (170) from the immediate `Func<EffectHandle>` overload used by `capture05` (171–176); avoid changing that capture callback's semantics accidentally.

1. **Arm from existing authority.** Keep the current PHOTO/CAPTURE/travel prerequisites and actual scan requirement. Store an engine stop qualification or a radar scan qualification before launching, because both are transient edges. Reflection already has persistent prerequisite/distance eligibility. Start only when the relevant audience is ready and the existing four/eight-second quiet gate has elapsed.
2. **Play without writing `played`.** Track the owned sequence handle immediately so focus/pause/disable can stop it. Supply a closure over the immutable cue kind to `PlaySequence(condition: ...)`, rather than a closure over a mutable slot that becomes empty after commit. Do not change the EffectDirector clock or create another coroutine/timeline.
3. **Before the delivery checkpoint, interruption returns to armed.** Stop only that owned handle; reset its observed exposure and quiet gate. Retry from sequence time zero after the relevant audience returns and a fresh quiet interval; `startAtSeconds` belongs to the rock's established elapsed-time resume policy and would omit the preparation/context of an interrupted primary beat. A radar retry reuses the already-earned actual scan qualification and spends no second charge. An engine retry must again have stopped propulsion; a new sustained movement/stop may refresh its qualification. A brief viewer/focus interruption must not permanently consume a scare or trigger a burst of retries every LateUpdate.
4. **Commit at a meaningful observed checkpoint while still playing.** A concrete starting policy for later review is: engine real voice advances at least 0.35 s with Helm/stopped propulsion; radar actual FalseRadarSweep is visible on the Radar surface across observations and its real echo voice advances; reflection actual ProceduralReflection is visible on the tilted Helm surface across observations and its accompanying movement voice advances. A short continuous exposure budget (for example 0.25 s for radar/reflection) must begin at the first qualifying observation, not be credited retroactively from the whole Tick delta. If visibility/preset/focus breaks, reset the budget. Exact exposure tuning remains conditional on the baseline. Merely crossing an authored timestamp or seeing a valid request is insufficient.
5. **Failed durable publication retries publication, not content.** After the checkpoint, retain an in-memory `deliveredAwaitingCommit` stage. Call `CommitZoneThreeCue` only while the loop permits commits; a failure retains that stage and retries with a bounded interval, preserving `LastError`, instead of replaying the delivered sound/visual or writing on every frame. On success, increment the existing beat counter once and clear the transient slot. The remaining owned sequence can recover normally; later interruption cancels its tail, and the durable receipt prevents future retriggers. The counters continue to mean successful cue-receipt events, not number of playback attempts.
6. **Terminal cleanup is explicit.** Zone change, death, timeline restore, owner disable and rock destruction cancel the candidate and owned handle. Pause/focus/panel loss are temporary interruptions if the candidate has not reached delivery. `ResetPresentation` and `StopOwned` currently share cleanup paths, so separate “stop playback, keep eligible candidate” from “drop candidate for a replaced/terminal timeline.” Process pending cancellation/status before `Tick` returns through its existing `!Safe`/`!Unobstructed` branches. Never let a candidate from a replaced snapshot commit into the new snapshot.

For a deferred radar receipt, preserve its **original real scan distance**, not the distance when a later retry/checkpoint publishes. Today `ExpeditionLoop.CommitZoneThreeCue` (69–78) captures `Navigation.DistanceTravelled` at immediate publication. The narrow possible seam is an optional, finite/nonnegative `observedDistance` argument for radar only, with existing callers retaining default behavior; candidate commit passes its stored scan distance. This preserves the authored 100-unit acoustic-passage rule without a save-schema change. Do not accidentally move passage onset by delaying the receipt.

This transient design guarantees retry only within the same live timeline. If Continue occurs **before** delivery, the pending candidate is deliberately dropped, no false receipt is written, and normal gameplay eligibility must be earned again (reflection remains eligible if the radar receipt was already durable). Persisting an uncompleted radar scan qualification across Continue without another charge would require an additive saved pending state and is a separate larger decision. Do not claim that stronger guarantee from a controller-only transient patch. Existing receipts remain honored; there is no migration that replays old saves' recorded cues.

If the selected design instead requires a receipt only after *full natural sequence completion*, the current API cannot establish that outcome. It would need a narrowly tested Director terminal-outcome signal and evidence that required peak children actually ran; a bare Completed callback still cannot prove delivery after a hitch skips the peak. Prefer the checkpoint seam above if the baseline need is simply to avoid consuming unseen peaks.

## Conditional refinement: saved radar opportunity without new schema

Still conditional on the completed actual audit. This refines the transient-only limitation above if preserving the earned radar opportunity through Continue is selected. No Assets/Unity change has been made.

### Saved labels and compatibility

Use one additional string constant such as `ZoneThreePresentationState.RadarOpportunity = "radar.opportunity"` in the existing `played` list. Its meaning is **an observed qualifying real scan was durably remembered; the false-radar presentation has not necessarily been delivered**. Keep `"radar"` as the no-replay receipt for the primary beat, published at the new delivery checkpoint. Expose a nonserialized convenience property if useful: `HasRadarOpportunity => Has(RadarOpportunity) || Has("radar")`.

This does not add a serialized field or require a save-version change. `ZoneThreePresentationState.Normalize` removes only blank/duplicate labels and validates the existing distances; it does not whitelist receipt strings. `ExpeditionSaveStore.Copy` is a JsonUtility roundtrip (`ExpeditionSave.cs:112`), and read normalization covers current, day-start and each Journal checkpoint (`137–165`, `198–215`). `MissionLayoutMigration` modifies mission progress, not the ZoneThree presentation list. The marker therefore follows normal snapshot/rollback authority rather than living in a process-global cache.

Legacy `"radar"` receipts remain respected with their existing stored `radarDistance`, even without the new opportunity marker. Do not create an opportunity for them, change their anchor or replay them. They remain evidence of the *old accepted-start policy*, not retrospective proof that the new delivery checkpoint was reached. Baseline/final reports must name the build/policy when interpreting the same label. Existing engine/reflection labels need the same historical distinction.

### Narrow publication and playback flow

1. Observe the real post-L2-SEND charged scan on the Radar audience; latch its first observed navigation distance in memory. Arming itself must not start an effect, consume another charge or imply delivery. `RadarDisplay.IsScanning` lasts **2.0 seconds**, and hiding Radar calls `ResetScanState`; if a four-second quiet rule is chosen, do not wait for that rule before remembering the actual scan. Separate scan opportunity from later presentation readiness. This is a conditional behavior change that requires the actual audit's approval.
2. Publish `"radar.opportunity"` with that observation distance **before starting the primary sequence**. If publication fails, keep an in-memory opportunity-awaiting-publication stage and retry the save with a bounded interval; no false-radar effect has played, so retries cannot duplicate it. Keep the first distance, rather than relatching every frame of the same scan. If publication is temporarily blocked, do not spend another scan to recover it.
3. If the saved opportunity exists and `"radar"` does not, reconstruct the armed radar candidate after Continue. It may wait until the Radar panel, focus, safety and quiet conditions are suitable; it does not require or initiate another real scan. Do not globally open Radar or force the viewer to stay. If the chosen quiet rule measures time after a sequence ceases, reset that observation clock while a primary/other authored sequence is active and when it ends/cancels; time spent inside the preceding beat must not count as post-beat quiet.
4. Play using the audience-safe conditional sequence and defer `"radar"` until the actual checkpoint. On successful delivery publication, retain `"radar.opportunity"` and its anchor; the delivered marker disarms all retries. The opportunity marker is intentionally not a presentation event counter. Keep the acoustic passage/reflection prerequisite on **`Has("radar")`**, not `HasRadarOpportunity`, so an armed but unseen false-radar cue does not advance the horror chain.

The minimal API seam is `CommitZoneThreeCue(string id, float? observationDistance = null)` with finite/nonnegative validation for an explicitly supplied distance. In the copied candidate, write `radarDistance` when adding `"radar.opportunity"`. When adding `"radar"`, preserve that anchor if the opportunity marker exists; for older immediate callers without an opportunity, retain the current default of using the supplied observation distance or current actual navigation distance. Detect presence by the marker, not `radarDistance > 0`—zero is a legitimate first-scan distance. The controller must not arm again after `"radar"`, and the API can reject adding an opportunity after a delivered/legacy radar receipt. Existing `capture05` callers preserve their original distance behavior.

After a visible/audible checkpoint but failed **delivery** publication, keep `deliveredAwaitingCommit` in memory and retry only the receipt. Do not restart the sequence when its handle ends, and do not fall back into the “saved opportunity, no radar receipt” launch path while this stage exists. Clear that memory only on a replaced/terminal timeline, or on successful publication. This provides no duplicate effect during continued operation of the same timeline.

There is an unavoidable boundary: if delivery publication never succeeds and the process/timeline is reloaded, durable storage contains only the opportunity and cannot prove whether its effect was already delivered. The saved opportunity can offer the beat again. Exactly-once delivery across **failed durable write plus reload** cannot be guaranteed by this label scheme; recording another delivered marker would fail on the same unavailable storage. Do not claim or test that stronger guarantee. After a successful delivered receipt, Continue must suppress replay as usual.
